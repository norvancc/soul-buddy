Privacy

Soul Buddy works between your iPhone or iPad and your paired Mac on your local network. No account is required.

Data stays on your devices

Soul Buddy does not send your data to the developer. It includes no advertising, tracking, analytics service, or automatic crash upload. It does not send your prompts, code, chat history, or agent responses to the phone.

Local pairing and activity

Your devices exchange pairing information, device identifiers, companion activity states, and selected theme files. Tapping a companion can ask your paired Mac to open its associated app. Pairing credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Theme files and display preferences are stored locally.

Local Network access

This permission lets the app discover and connect to your Mac. Connections use your local network and are not encrypted in this version. Use a trusted private network.

Your controls

Hold the companion display for two seconds and choose Forget Mac to remove the saved pairing from the phone. You can also remove a phone in the Mac app. Removing the app deletes its local files; use Forget Mac first to remove its saved pairing credential.

Support

If you contact the developer through the support page, you choose what information to share. Do not include passwords, pairing codes, credentials, or private code in a public issue.
