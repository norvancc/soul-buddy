Let’s get connected.

Soul Buddy is developed by Norvan. For help, open a support issue.

What you need

A Mac running macOS 14 or later, an iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 15 or later, and both Soul Buddy apps. Keep your devices on the same trusted private network. A supported coding-agent integration on the Mac provides live activity.

Pair your devices

  1. Download the Mac app, unzip it, and move Soul Buddy to Applications.
  2. Open the Mac app and follow the integration setup.
  3. Open the phone app and allow Local Network access.
  4. On the Mac, open Devices → Add device. Enter the phone’s six-digit code and connect.

Make yourself at home

Swipe left for the face and right for the world. Tap a companion to return to its app on your Mac. Tap empty space to brighten the clock. Hold the display for two seconds to open its controls. Choose or import themes on your Mac.

If the connection is missing

Keep both apps open and check Local Network permission. Guest Wi-Fi, network isolation, firewalls, and some VPN configurations can prevent discovery. Reopen the phone app for a fresh code. If agent activity is missing, check Integrations on your Mac and start a new agent session after setup.

Report a problem

Include app and operating-system versions and the steps to reproduce the problem. GitHub issues are public: omit passwords, pairing codes, credentials, private code, and chat content.
