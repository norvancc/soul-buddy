A little presence.
Right beside you.

Soul Buddy turns your AI coding-agent sessions into animated companions on your iPhone or iPad. Keep a quiet world beside your Mac, or swipe to an expressive full-screen face.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
iPhone and iPad version 1.7.6 is awaiting App Store review. It will be free when approved.

Pair once. Stay close.

Open both apps on the same trusted private network. Enter the phone’s six-digit code on your Mac. No Soul Buddy account is required.

A companion for each session.

See your agents think, work, wait, and celebrate. Tap a companion to return to its app on your Mac. Choose themes from the Mac app and bring them to your phone.

Create a world of your own.

Mac 1.8.7 includes a theme creation Skill for Codex and Claude Code. Give your agent reference materials and an idea, then import the finished theme into Soul Buddy.
